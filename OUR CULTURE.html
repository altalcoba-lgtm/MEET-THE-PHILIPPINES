FILIPINO CULTURE AND TRADITIONS 🇵🇭

1. Filipino Hospitality

Filipinos are known for their warmth, kindness, and hospitality. They welcome
visitors with friendly smiles, generosity, and respect, making tourists feel at home wherever they go.

2. Festivals and Celebrations

The Philippines is famous for its colorful festivals, lively street dances, and exciting celebrations.
Festivals like Sinulog, Ati-Atihan, and Panagbenga showcase Filipino creativity, traditions, and community spirit.

3. Traditional Food and Cuisine

Filipino cuisine is a delicious reflection of the country's culture and history.
Popular dishes such as adobo, sinigang, lechon, and kare-kare allow visitors to experience the unique flavors of the Philippines.

4. Filipino Traditions and Values

Family, respect, and unity are important parts of Filipino life. Traditions like pagmamano, bayanihan, and family
gatherings show the importance of respecting elders, helping others, and staying connected with loved ones.
